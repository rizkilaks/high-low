How to play
  1. 9 rounds — the top 2 scores win.
  2. Each round deals a point prize (+ or −). Everyone plays ONE card from their hand (1–10), within 60 seconds.
  3. HIGHEST: closest above the prize wins. An odd number of Reverse cards flips the round to LOWEST: closest below wins.
  4. Two players playing the same card value are both voided — only survivors can win. No survivors = no winner, prize burned.
  5. Pass burns a card but keeps it hidden — you are forced to reveal next round. The round's start seat cannot pass.
  6. Reverse: one per player for the whole game.
  7. Negative prize with voided players: the winner may gift the negative to one of them.
